### `~/about`

Miracle is a small, senior-led studio founded in 2026. We take products from a
blank page to production: architecture, design, build, deploy and maintenance.
We care about performance, clean systems and the details — animation, DX and
the parts users never see but always feel.

- **Founder & portfolio** → [1llusion.dev](https://1llusion.dev/)
- **Studio & work** → [miracledev.cc](https://miracledev.cc/)
- **Community & enquiries** → our [Discord](https://miracledev.cc/)
